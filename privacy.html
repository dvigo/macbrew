Privacy Policy

MacBrew is built on a fundamental principle: Privacy by Design. We respect your privacy and do not track you.

🔒
100% Cookie-Free & Tracker-Free

MacBrew uses zero cookies, zero local storage tracking, zero third-party analytics scripts, and zero advertising trackers.

1. No Cookies of Any Kind

We do not store, place, or access cookies on your browser. Because we do not use tracking cookies or personal data profiling, there are no annoying cookie banners or consent popups required on MacBrew.

2. 100% Client-Side State & URL Sharing

Your app selections stay entirely in your web browser. When you share a setup link, your selected packages are encoded strictly within the URL query string (e.g. ?apps=visual-studio-code,docker,slack). No database or server-side user profiles are created.

3. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect names, email addresses, IP logs, or device telemetry. MacBrew is a pure utility designed to generate commands and Brewfiles for your macOS terminal.

4. Open & Transparent

MacBrew is fully transparent and open source. You can inspect the source code anytime in our official GitHub repository.
